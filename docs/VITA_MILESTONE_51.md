# Sight City: Underworld — Milestone 51

## Mission 16: The Cleanup

Story-only milestone. Online remains paused.

- Meet Mayja after the hostage rescue.
- Make one permanent target choice: **Witnesses**, **Evidence**, **Crew Base**, or **Bank Archive**.
- Neutralize the selected Project Sight cleanup operation.
- Survive Project Sight retaliation at Wanted Level 4.
- Recover the **Master Ledger**, revealing the **Sight City Central Reserve**.
- Dash survives; the permanent choice is retained for later campaign consequences.
- Reward: **$175,000 + 15,000 XP**.
- Unlocks **Mission 17 — The Ledger**.

PS Vita remains the priority platform. Online/cross-generation development stays paused until Story is playable and tested on real PS Vita hardware.
