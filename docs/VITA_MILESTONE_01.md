# Milestone 01 – PS Vita street prototype

Implemented:
- Vita controller backend (dual analog + buttons)
- Third-person camera
- On-foot player avatar
- Enter/exit vehicle interaction
- Driveable prototype car
- Procedural city blocks around the player
- Perspective-projected 3D-style geometry
- Lightweight HUD and 0–5 wanted level core
- Vita-focused 30 fps configuration

Next milestones:
1. Correct rotated vehicle mesh and character facing
2. Collision against buildings/curbs
3. NPC pedestrians + lightweight traffic pool
4. Road/path graph and police spawning
5. Missions/checkpoints, money and garage
6. Text/font HUD, pause map, audio and save data
7. Separate rendering backends for PS3, Xbox 360 and PS4
