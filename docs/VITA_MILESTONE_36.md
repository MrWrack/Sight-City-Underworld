# Milestone 36 - The Long Way Home

Story Mission 4 is now implemented as a playable mission-state flow.

- Escape the Industrial Zone.
- Reach the highway.
- Police join the chase and Wanted escalates.
- Enter the mountain tunnels.
- Break line of sight and lose the pursuit.
- Reach the North Countryside mountain garage.
- Reward: $18,000 + 1,200 XP.
- Unlocks Mission 5: Our First Score.

Online development remains paused until Story is tested on physical PS Vita hardware.
