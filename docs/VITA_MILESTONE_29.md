# Milestone 29 — Flyable Aircraft (Story Mode)

Adds the first flyable-aircraft gameplay layer for Story Mode.

- 16 established fictional aircraft in the aircraft catalog.
- Player-control physics foundation: taxi, throttle, steering, takeoff, pitch, roll, yaw/turning, flight and landing transition.
- Terrain-height contact and basic crash damage.
- Aircraft classes tune takeoff/max speed: light aircraft, jets, airliners, cargo and military aircraft.
- Designed to fly across the existing 120 x 120 km Sight City world.
- Story Mode remains the priority; Online is intentionally not developed in this milestone.

This remains a gameplay/physics prototype. 3D aircraft meshes, animation, runway navigation, advanced aerodynamics, audio and final Vita rendering are future work.
