# Sight City: Underworld — Vita Milestone 52

## Mission 17 — The Ledger
Story-first implementation of the Master Ledger operation.

Objectives:
1. Meet Mayja and review the Master Ledger targets.
2. Raid the Harbor / Port financial node.
3. Raid the Airport transfer node.
4. Raid the South Hills shell-account node.
5. Trace the Downtown money trail.
6. Deliver the complete ledger package to Mayja.

The four field targets are designed as order-flexible Story objectives. Completion requires all four. The recovered financial picture confirms Sight City Central Reserve as the next major target.
