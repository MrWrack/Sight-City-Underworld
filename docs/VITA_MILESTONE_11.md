# Milestone 11 — Separate Story / Online campaigns + Online Character Creator

- Story Mode uses a fixed protagonist and Story-only campaign tree.
- Online uses a separately customizable player character.
- Online appearance fields: face, hair, hair color, skin tone, body build, top, bottoms, shoes, accessory.
- Story missions cannot start in Online and Online missions cannot start in Story.
- Story and Online character profiles are designed for separate save files/progression.
- Added starter Story chapters/heist and separate Online jobs/heist/race.

This is the gameplay/data foundation. A visual Vita character-creator screen and full 3D character meshes are later presentation/content work.
