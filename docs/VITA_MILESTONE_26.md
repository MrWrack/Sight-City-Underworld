# City Limits — Milestone 26: Seasons & Dynamic Weather

Story-first weather foundation for Sight City.

- Four seasons: Winter, Spring, Summer, Autumn.
- Deterministic day weather: clear, clouds, rain, heavy rain, fog, light/heavy snow, snowstorm.
- Altitude-aware temperature, so Mount Ridge can keep snow while low areas melt.
- Accumulating/melting snow cover and wet roads.
- Wet/snow road-grip multiplier for vehicle physics integration.
- Evening/night sky becomes deep blue; clouds darken to blue-grey with the M25 time-of-day lighting.
- Visibility and wind values for rendering, traffic AI and future particles.
- Designed for Vita: weather state is cheap; renderer can use capped particles and material swaps.
- Online remains postponed until Story is playable/testable.
