# Milestone 07 - Businesses, Banks & Economy
- Purchasable businesses with separate business accounts.
- Deposit/withdraw money between player and owned business.
- Daily revenue and operating costs.
- Business loans, debt warnings and bankruptcy.
- Banks are world locations and map-marker types.
- Region Economic Health can be temporarily disrupted by scripted bank/heist events and recovers over time.
- Business mission marker type added for future mission scripting.
- Designed as shared gameplay logic for Story/Online; save/network persistence is the next integration layer.
