# Sight City: Underworld — Milestone 42

## Story Mission 7: Wrong Place, Wrong Time

M42 adds the seventh Story Mode mission and keeps Online development paused.

Mission flow:
1. Meet Rhys for an Industrial Zone job.
2. Enter the job site.
3. Investigate the suspicious loading yard.
4. A rival gang ambush starts — they already knew the location.
5. Survive the attack.
6. Rhys arrives and saves Dash, keeping the betrayal reveal ambiguous.
7. Escape the Industrial Zone.
8. Debrief Mayja, who concludes that an insider is feeding information to the rivals.

Reward: **$35,000 + 3,000 XP**.
Unlock: **Mission 8 — Behind My Back**.

Story continuity rule: Rhys is still Dash's best friend at this point. His betrayal is not revealed in M42; the rescue is genuine and deliberately preserves uncertainty.

PS Vita priorities remain unchanged: Story first, Vita build/hardware testing before any further Online work.
