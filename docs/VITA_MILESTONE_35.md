# Milestone 35 - Easy Money + Dynamic World Hooks

Story-first milestone for Sight City: Underworld.

- Mission 3 `Easy Money`: Harbor Docks, electronics container, container truck theft, rival ambush/chase, delivery.
- Reward: $12,500 + 800 XP; unlocks Mission 4 `The Long Way Home`.
- Dynamic incident placement for crashes, robberies, knife attacks, fights and fires. Recent locations are rejected within 250 m and a rolling history is retained.
- Character meeting map markers carry character, location and time window. Active meetings pulse; Rhys prototype is 20:00-23:00.
- Speedometer setting supports km/h (default) and mph, including conversion from physics speed and save/load persistence.
- Online development remains intentionally paused until Story is tested on real PS Vita hardware.
