# Milestone 10 - Mission System

Adds a shared mission framework for Story Mode and Sight City Online.

- Categories: Story, Side, Race, Heist, Delivery, Police/Escape, Business, Vehicle Challenge, Aircraft, Stunt, Co-op Online.
- Ordered objectives with progress tracking.
- Checkpoint and restart-from-checkpoint support.
- Failure/completion states.
- Rewards: money, XP and string-based unlock hooks.
- Online-only mission gating.
- Lightweight text save/load for mission progression.
- Starter Sight City mission definitions for integration/testing.

This is the gameplay framework. Full scripted cutscenes, NPC mission actors, race route geometry, heist scenes and network replication are later content/system passes.
