# Milestone 13 - In-game phone foundation

Adds the shared in-game phone architecture for Story and Online.

Apps: Sight Shop, Properties, Garage, Bank, Businesses, Missions and Map.
Sight Shop has categorized purchasable home/property items and an inventory purchase path. This is a lightweight gameplay/data foundation; full Vita phone UI, property placement UI and network-backed Online services come in later milestones.
