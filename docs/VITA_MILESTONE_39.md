# Milestone 39 — Combat & Weapon Gameplay

Story-first combat foundation for Sight City: Underworld.

Implemented:
- equipped weapon + magazine state
- reload from WeaponInventory reserve ammo
- shot/hit resolution and range falloff
- headshot multiplier
- armor absorption for player/NPCs
- recoil value for camera integration
- NPC combat intents: engage, take cover, flank, flee, downed
- civilians flee from nearby armed threats
- witnessed gunfire returns a crime-report flag for Wanted/Emergency integration
- no Online implementation; Online remains paused until Story is tested on real PS Vita

Next integration targets: Vita HUD/weapon wheel rendering, animation/audio hooks, cover geometry queries, Wanted dispatch, and Mission 5: Our First Score.
