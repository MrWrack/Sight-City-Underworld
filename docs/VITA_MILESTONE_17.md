# Milestone 17 - Vehicle Shops & Dealerships 2.0

Adds a cross-platform gameplay foundation for vehicle dealerships and online vehicle purchasing.

- Dealer stock for cars, motorcycles, boats, aircraft and used vehicles.
- Test-drive sessions with a time limit.
- Buy a vehicle and select an owned garage for delivery.
- Used stock supports lower condition and lower purchase price.
- Internet/phone purchase hook uses the same stock and garage-delivery path.
- Vehicle customization remains attached to the owned vehicle.
- Tire smoke color customization is now stored per vehicle and charged as a customization purchase.
- Compatible purchased vehicles can be used later by the heist getaway-vehicle system.
- Story and Online continue to use separate GarageSystem instances/profile saves.

PS Vita remains data/logic-first: dealership screens should render only visible rows and avoid loading full vehicle assets at once.
