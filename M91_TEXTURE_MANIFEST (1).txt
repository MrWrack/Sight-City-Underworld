M91 Vita texture set (texture-ready)

Recommended small indexed/optimized source textures:
- asphalt_64.png       64x64
- concrete_64.png      64x64
- grass_64.png         64x64
- brick_64.png         64x64
- windows_64.png       64x64
- roof_64.png          64x64

M91 renderer adds the 3D geometry and NPC/AI layer first.
Do not load PNGs directly every frame. Decode once at startup / streamed asset load,
reuse GPU textures, and release them when the streamed region unloads.

The current M91 ZIP intentionally remains build-compatible with the working M90
renderer without introducing an untested image decoder dependency.
